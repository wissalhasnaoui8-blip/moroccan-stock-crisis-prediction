# Moroccan Stock Crisis Prediction: Data Sources

## Market data

- Casablanca Stock Exchange official reports
- MASI / MADEX / sector indices historical data
- Yahoo Finance for public market series when available
- Bloomberg / Refinitiv for institutional coverage (licensed)

## French textual data

- L'Economiste
- Finances News Hebdo
- Les Eco
- Economic and business sections of Moroccan media
- Bank Al-Maghrib speeches and official releases
- Government and institutional communication related to finance, debt, currency, and policy

## Macroeconomic data

- Bank Al-Maghrib
- Haut Commissariat au Plan (HCP)
- World Bank API
- IMF API and World Economic Outlook
- OECD stats
- Moroccan customs and trade statistics

## Notes

For French-language text, a practical strategy is to crawl and parse article metadata and content from a curated list of Moroccan and French news portals, then label themes such as:

- macroeconomy
- monetary policy
- political risk
- exchange rate
- debt and fiscal policy
- market liquidity
- sector stress
